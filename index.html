Privacy & limits

Scanning and redaction run entirely in your browser. Text, PDFs, and images never leave this device. PDF support extracts text only (via pdf.js from CDN). Image OCR uses Tesseract.js (English only) loaded from a CDN on first use, including the eng traineddata — after that it can be cached by your browser. Visual black-box redaction maps PII substrings to OCR word boxes (fuzzy); handwriting and non-English text are unreliable. Detectors are pattern-based — expect false positives/negatives. Credit cards use a Luhn check; SSNs match common formats only.
