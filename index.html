Privacy & limits

Scanning and redaction run entirely in your browser. Text and PDFs never leave this device. PDF support extracts text only (via pdf.js from CDN); visual black-box redaction of PDF pages is out of scope. Detectors are pattern-based — expect false positives/negatives. Credit cards use a Luhn check; SSNs match common formats only.
